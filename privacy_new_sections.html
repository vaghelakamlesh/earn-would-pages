Leaderboard Display Name

Your display name is masked (e.g., "Kam***") on public weekly leaderboards to protect your privacy. Your email address is never shown on leaderboards or shared with other users.

Notifications

We send in-app and system notifications regarding your account activity, daily bonus reminders, and redeem request status updates. You can manage notification preferences in your device settings.

Google Sign-In Data

If you choose to sign in using Google, we collect your name, email address, and Google profile ID to authenticate your account and set up your user profile.

Device and Advertising Identifiers

Google AdMob collects advertising IDs (e.g., AAID), IP addresses, and device metrics to serve, measure, and verify rewarded video advertisements. See Google's Advertising Policies for details.

Data Retained After Account Deletion

When you delete your account, your profile, points, and history are removed. To prevent returning user referral fraud, ban evasion, and duplicate payout abuse, we retain a one-way cryptographic SHA-256 hash of your email address and recorded payout IDs (UPI ID / email).

Data Retention

Active account data is stored securely in Supabase for as long as your account remains active. Completed and pending withdrawal records are retained as necessary for accounting, compliance, and fraud prevention.
