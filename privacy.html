Privacy Policy

Last updated: 06-10-2026. This policy applies to the Earn Would Android app ("the app").

What we collect

Account data: your email address, the name you choose, and your password (stored securely by our backend provider, Supabase, never in plain text).

Activity data: points you earn, daily bonus and spin history, invite code and who you invited, and your redeem requests.

Payment details you give us: the UPI ID or Google Play email you enter when you redeem a reward. We use it only to send that reward.

Messages: anything you write in the Contact form.

Advertising data: the app shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and similar device information to show and measure ads. See Google's advertising policy.

How we use it

To run your account, add the points you earn, review and pay redeem requests, prevent fraud and duplicate accounts, answer your messages, and show ads.

Who we share it with

Supabase (hosting and database) and Google AdMob (ads). We do not sell your personal data.

Age

The app is for people aged 18 and over.

Your choices

You can delete your account inside the app (Me > Delete my account), or see how to request deletion. When an account is deleted, its profile, points history, messages and redeem records are removed.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what the app can change on its own: points are added only by our server after checks.
